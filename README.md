What Happened to Alex?
Case Overview

Case Name: The Alex Investigation
Case Type: Digital Forensics / Incident Investigation
Status: Open — Investigation Activity
Objective: Determine what happened to Alex by analyzing the digital evidence provided in this repository.

Something happened to Alex.

The circumstances surrounding his disappearance/incidence are unclear, and there is no single piece of evidence that directly explains what occurred. Your task is to examine the available digital evidence, connect the clues, establish a timeline, and ultimately deduce what happened to Alex.

This repository contains the evidence needed to conduct the investigation.
