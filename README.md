## Case Overview

Something happened to **Alex**, but the circumstances are unclear.

Your task is to investigate the digital evidence provided in this repository and **deduce what happened to him**.

Look for connections between:

* Images and visual clues
* Messages and communications
* Timestamps and timelines
* Hidden or unusual information

## Objective

Reconstruct the sequence of events and answer:

> **What happened to Alex?**

Your conclusion should be supported by evidence and logical deductions.

## ⚠️ Important Note

Some images in this activity are **AI-generated or digitally created solely for educational purposes**.

They are fictional and should **not be treated as authentic photographs or real-world evidence**.

Focus on the clues, metadata, timestamps, and relationships between the provided evidence.
